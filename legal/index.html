Privacy policy

Your replay data stays on your PC. ChestTracker analyses your Fortnite replay files locally. The chest history, settings and statistics are stored on your computer and are not uploaded to us.

What the program sends over the internet

  • Updates and event data: it downloads a small version file and the Treasure Hour schedule from our website (hosted on GitHub Pages). No personal data is sent.
  • Weekly reward names and pictures: it asks the public cosmetics database fortnite-api.com. No personal data is sent.
  • Licence check: it sends your licence key and a name for your PC (a code derived from your computer, not your name) to Lemon Squeezy to confirm and activate the licence.
